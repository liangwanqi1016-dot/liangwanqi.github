Collection

Names, dates and materials follow the museum’s Spring 2024 bulletin.

Read the object labels below. Original photographs are available in the museum bulletin linked from each entry.

Traditional pottery

Hoeryong jar

회령단지

Date
Japanese colonial period
Material
Earthenware

This water jar bears the glaze characteristic of Hoeryong in the northern Korean peninsula. Its shape and glaze reflect local pottery traditions and household water storage.

European porcelain

Bernardaud vase

베르나르도 화병

Date
20th century · France
Material
Porcelain

Still lifes adorn both faces of the flattened vase. Blue-and-white vessels and red fruit stand against a black ground, with linear decoration at the rim and edges.

European porcelain

Bernardaud decorative plate

베르나르도 장식 접시

Date
20th century · France
Material
Porcelain

A still life decorates the inside of the plate. The bulletin identifies the image as transfer-printed. Compare it with the companion vase.

European porcelain

Blue Flowers vase

에밀 갈레 ‘푸른 꽃’ 화병

Date
19th century · France
Material
Glass

Émile Gallé used amber-coloured opaque cameo glass and blue enamel to depict the floral motif.

History of education

Natural Science, Grade 5, Term 2

자연 5-2

Date
1 September 1963
Material
Paper

Issued by the Ministry of Education under the second national curriculum. The bulletin records that UNESCO and UNKRA donated the printing equipment after the war.

Viewing stones

Seal-shaped viewing stone

물개 물형석

Date
Collected on 20 December 1983
Material
Natural stone

Collected in the mountains of Danyang and donated by Han Gyeong-su. Its natural outline suggests a seal, illustrating the role of imagination in stone appreciation.

Local history

Han Jun’s stone case and porcelain epitaph

한준 석함 · 청화백자묘지명

Date
17th century
Material
Stone and blue-and-white porcelain

These objects associated with Han Jun (1542–1601) were unearthed during the relocation of his tomb in 2013 and donated in March 2024.
