Permanent exhibitions

Five themes across six galleries trace objects, lives and cultures through time.

Onggi Galleries 1 and 2

옹기

Traditional pottery

The Jeommal kiln site in Yeowol-dong introduces the making of onggi and their use for storing water, grain and fermented foods.

In this gallery

  • The Jeommal kiln through time
  • Clay, patterns and glazes
  • Forming and firing
  • Regional forms and fermentation

Bucheon Local History Gallery

부천향토역사

Local history

From prehistoric finds at Gogang-dong to late Joseon funerary objects, the gallery follows archaeological discoveries in Bucheon.

In this gallery

  • Prehistoric finds from Gogang-dong
  • Objects from Princess Hwayu’s tomb
  • Local archaeology and epitaphs
