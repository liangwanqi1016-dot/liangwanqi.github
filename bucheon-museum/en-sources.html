Building photograph: Bucheon Museums website.

Five themes in one museum

The museum reopened in 2021, bringing together education, European porcelain, viewing stones, pottery and local history. Its six rooms include two onggi galleries and one gallery for each of the other themes.

This independent exhibition draws on the supplied Bucheon City Museum collection dossier. Please consult the museum for visiting arrangements and current exhibitions.
